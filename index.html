Image Search
📷 How image search works:

Tap the camera icon on the Search tab. The app will open Google Lens with your photo. Google Lens will identify the pin — copy that name, then come back here and paste it into the search box.

Once your eBay API key arrives, the app will use AI to attempt automatic pin identification without leaving the app.
